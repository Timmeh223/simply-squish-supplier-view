This policy explains how Simply Squish collects, uses, shares and protects personal information.

Who controls your information

Simply Squish is operated by [LEGAL BUSINESS NAME REQUIRED] in Australia. Contact us at support@simplysquish.com.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the store, we may collect:

  • your name, email address, phone number, delivery and billing address;
  • order details, product choices, payment status, refunds and delivery records;
  • messages, support requests, photos or videos you send about an order;
  • device, browser, IP address, cookie and website-use information; and
  • marketing choices and records of consent or unsubscribe requests.

Our payment provider processes payment details. We do not need to receive or store your complete card number.

Why we use it

We use personal information to:

  • provide the store and process orders;
  • take payment and prevent fraud;
  • arrange fulfilment, international delivery and tracking;
  • provide support and handle returns, refunds, claims and disputes;
  • keep records, meet legal duties and protect our rights;
  • improve the store and measure performance; and
  • send marketing only where permitted and according to your choices.

Who receives it

We share only the information reasonably needed with service providers that help us operate, such as:

  • website hosting and store technology providers;
  • payment and fraud-prevention providers;
  • order fulfilment, warehouse, customs, carrier and delivery providers;
  • email and customer-support providers;
  • analytics or advertising providers, if activated; and
  • professional advisers, regulators or authorities where required.

Some fulfilment and technology providers may be outside Australia and outside your country. This can include providers in [COUNTRIES MUST BE CONFIRMED BEFORE PUBLICATION]. We do not give customer order details to fulfilment partners for their own unrelated marketing.

Cookies and advertising

The store may use essential cookies needed for security, preferences, the bag and checkout. Any analytics, advertising pixels, personalised advertising or non-essential cookies must be listed here after they are actually configured. Where consent is required, we will ask before using those technologies.

Marketing

Order and service messages are not marketing. We send promotional messages only where we have a lawful basis, and every promotional email will include a way to unsubscribe. Unsubscribing from marketing does not stop necessary order or safety messages.

How long we keep information

We keep information only for as long as reasonably needed for orders, support, fraud prevention, tax, accounting, dispute and legal duties. Exact periods must be recorded before this policy is published: [RETENTION SCHEDULE REQUIRED].

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect personal information. No online service can promise absolute security. Access is limited to people and providers who need it for their role.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or a copy of personal information, or to object to or limit some uses. You may also withdraw marketing consent. These rights can have legal exceptions.

Send a request to support@simplysquish.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Privacy complaints

Contact us first at support@simplysquish.com and explain the concern. We will acknowledge and review it within a reasonable time. This does not stop you from contacting a privacy regulator where you have that right.

Children

The store is for purchases by adults. We do not knowingly ask children to create an account or provide personal information without appropriate involvement from a parent or guardian.

Updates

We will update this policy when our information-handling practices change. The current version and update date will remain available on the website.
