Returns & order help

Simply Squish customer draft
Last updated: 22 September 2026
Status: Draft for review. Do not publish yet.

Please contact Simply Squish first if something is wrong. You do not need to contact our fulfilment partners.

Change of mind

We do not currently plan to accept returns or exchanges when you simply change your mind, no longer want the product, find it elsewhere for less or choose the wrong item.

This change-of-mind rule does not limit any right you have when an item is faulty, damaged, unsafe, materially different from its description, incorrect or missing.

Damaged, leaking, faulty, incorrect or missing items

Contact us as soon as reasonably possible and preferably within 14 days after delivery. An earlier report makes it easier to preserve carrier and packing evidence. A 14-day contact request does not remove any longer right that applies under law.

Please include:

  • your order number;
  • a clear description of the problem;
  • photos or a short video showing the item and problem;
  • a photo of the parcel, delivery label and all items received; and
  • whether the outer parcel was damaged or opened.

Keep the item and packaging until we tell you whether they are needed for the review. Please do not send anything to an address found on the parcel without contacting us first.

We may request reasonable evidence so we can confirm the problem and prevent misuse. We will not demand evidence that is impossible or unnecessary. Once reviewed, we may offer a replacement, refund, repair where practical, or another remedy required by law. If only one item in a mixed order is affected, we can resolve that item without cancelling the unaffected items where practical.

Lost parcels and serious delays

If a parcel does not arrive within the stated delivery estimate, contact us with your order number. We may first investigate with the carrier. If the parcel is confirmed lost, cannot be delivered, or another remedy is required by law, we will offer the appropriate replacement or refund.

Cancelling or changing an order

Contact us immediately if you need to cancel or change an order. We can usually cancel an order while it remains unprocessed, but we cannot guarantee a change or cancellation once processing or fulfilment has begun.

If we agree to cancel an unshipped order, we will refund the cancelled amount to the original payment method. If we cannot dispatch within the promised time, we will give you the delay or cancellation choices described in our Shipping and Delivery Policy.

Returns we approve

Do not return a product until we give you written instructions. The return address may not be the address printed on the parcel.

For an approved return, we will explain:

  • what must be returned;
  • the correct return address;
  • the deadline;
  • who pays the return postage; and
  • what evidence of posting to keep.

If we confirm that a product problem is covered, we will pay or reimburse reasonable return costs where required by law. We do not charge a restocking fee for faulty, damaged, incorrect or materially misdescribed products.

Refund timing

Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method. We will confirm when we submit the refund. Your bank or card provider may take additional time to show it on your account.

Any refund will reflect the amount actually paid for the affected item or order, including delivery or tax amounts where the law or the circumstances require them. We do not replace a required cash or card refund with store credit unless you choose that option.

Your own mix

You may choose one or more available products. Any future bundle savings or free-shipping offer will be shown clearly before payment.

Misuse and dishonest claims

We may refuse a claim where the evidence shows the product was misused, intentionally damaged, materially altered after delivery, or the claim is fraudulent. We may also limit repeated abusive claims where the law allows. We will not use this section to reject a genuine problem without reviewing it fairly.

Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy excludes or limits rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. Simply Squish is responsible for resolving eligible problems with products it sells, even when another company helped fulfil or deliver the order.
