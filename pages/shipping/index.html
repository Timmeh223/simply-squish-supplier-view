Shipping & delivery

Simply Squish customer draft
Last updated: 22 September 2026
Status: Draft for review. Do not publish yet.

We want you to know what to expect before you order.

Where we deliver

We currently plan to accept orders for eligible addresses in the United States. Availability can depend on the product and delivery address. We may be unable to serve some remote locations, PO boxes, military addresses, US territories or other restricted addresses.

Your delivery options and any charge will be shown before payment. If we cannot confirm a suitable delivery option for your address, we will not accept the order.

Processing and delivery estimates

Our current planned estimate is:

  • processing before dispatch: 1 to 2 business days; and
  • estimated delivery after dispatch: 7 to 15 days.

These times are estimates, not guarantees. Weekends, public holidays, customs checks, severe weather, carrier disruption and address problems can cause delays. We will only show this estimate when we have a reasonable basis for it for the selected products and address.

How orders are fulfilled

We work with fulfilment and delivery partners, including partners outside the United States, to prepare and send orders directly to customers. Your parcel may therefore be sent internationally. Simply Squish remains your point of contact for order help.

Items in one order may arrive in more than one parcel. When multiple tracking numbers are available, we will send them by email or make them available with the order. A split shipment does not add a new delivery charge after you have paid.

Tracking

We will send tracking details to the email address used for the order when they become available. Tracking can take a few days to show its first scan.

Please contact us if tracking has not updated for an unusual period or the estimated delivery window has passed. We will review the carrier record and the order details before deciding the next step.

Correct delivery details

Please check your name, street address, apartment or unit number, city, state and ZIP code before paying. Contact us as soon as possible if something is wrong.

We will try to correct an address before processing begins, but changes are not guaranteed after an order enters fulfilment. If an incorrect or incomplete address supplied with the order causes a parcel to be returned, lost or redelivered, we may ask you to pay reasonable new delivery costs where the law allows. This does not limit your rights if the mistake was ours.

Delivery delays

If we learn that we cannot dispatch your order within the time promised at checkout, we will contact you with a revised dispatch date and give you the choice required by applicable law to accept the delay or cancel the affected unshipped items for a prompt refund. If we cannot fulfil an item, we will not silently substitute a different product.

Marked delivered but not received

If tracking says delivered but you cannot find the parcel:

1. check the delivery address in your confirmation;
2. check safe places, your household, neighbours and building staff;
3. contact the carrier if its tracking page offers a local delivery enquiry; and
4. contact Simply Squish promptly with your order number.

We may ask for information needed to investigate, such as confirmation from the local carrier. Tracking marked delivered is relevant evidence, but it does not automatically end our review. We will assess the available records and provide any remedy required by law.

Customs and import charges

Any duties, taxes or other mandatory charges collected from you will be shown before payment where required. We will not add an undisclosed handling fee after your order is accepted. If a government or carrier lawfully charges the recipient directly, we will explain that risk before payment when we know it applies.
