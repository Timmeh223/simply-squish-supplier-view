Care & safe play

Soft surfaces can show wear with regular squeezing. Keep your squish in its packaging or a pouch when you put it away.

A small sealed filling point can be part of how this squish is made. Avoid squeezing directly on that spot.

This is a toy, not food. Keep away from mouths, sharp objects, heat and pets. Stop using it if damaged or leaking. Ages 3+. Adult supervision is recommended. Follow the care instructions on the packaging.
